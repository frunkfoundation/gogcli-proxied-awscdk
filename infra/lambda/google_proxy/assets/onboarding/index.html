1. Enable APIs

Open each API and choose Enable. If it shows Manage, it is already enabled.

2. Set up branding, audience, and scopes

Open Branding ↗. If Google shows Get started, enter these values, choose External, agree to the policy, and choose Create.

    5. Connect your accounts

    For each account, choose its services and Connect. Google will ask you to choose that account and approve its permissions. Each account is connected after Google approval and the service checks finish.

    Calendar is optional. To add it to a connected account, select Calendar, review its API in step 4, then choose Reconnect / add selected service under Connection options and approve fresh Google consent.
